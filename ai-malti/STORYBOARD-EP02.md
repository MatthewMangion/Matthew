## The seven proverbs

All seven were checked against published sources (qawl.mt page titles or several independent sources). Idioms that could not be verified, such as *mill-ħabba qubba* and *kemm-il ras, kemm-il fehma*, were left out.

## Sound

- **Harmony:** the Andalusian cadence Dm–C–B♭–A, one full cycle per proverb, lands on the A-major that resolves to D minor at the loop point.
- **Instruments:**
  - Karplus-Strong nylon guitar (arpeggios and strums; tuning verified to within 0.2 cents).
  - A *żaqq*-like reed lead with a drone.
  - Tanbur *maqsum* rhythm.
  - Modern kick, clap and sub bass underneath.
- **Sound design:**
  - Ceramic tile clacks on every flip.
  - A countdown thump whose pitch climbs as the numbers fall.
  - A wooden knock on each word of the proverb.
  - Water drops tuned to the beat.
  - A draining tone for the lie's life bar, a stamp, and dome "poofs".
  - A reverse swell into the loop.
