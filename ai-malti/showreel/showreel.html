How it was built.

Engine

A deterministic Canvas 2D renderer where every frame is a pure function of time, so scenes render in parallel headless browsers. Motion blur is real: each frame averages 3–10 sub-frames in linear light. A WebGL2 finishing pass adds dual-filter bloom, chromatic aberration, glitch slicing, vignette and grain, then packs YUV 4:2:0 on the GPU for the encoder.

Type

Archivo's width axis runs 62–125%. Canvas can't animate width, so 64 fixed-width faces are registered and interpolated between. Instrument Serif italic carries the voice and JetBrains Mono the data, with every Maltese letter (ċ ġ ħ ż, għ, ie) set natively.

Sound

An original track synthesized in Python from oscillators and filters: kick, claps, supersaws, plucks, lead and more than 40 sound-design cues driven by the same timeline as the picture. It includes a tape-stop for the joke, crickets for the silence and a true-peak limiter.

Maltese details

The typographic map uses the real coastline of Malta, Gozo and Comino. The attention scene rests on a Maltese pun, since bank means both bank and bench. Colours come from the luzzu, and the eye of the luzzu is the attention head.
