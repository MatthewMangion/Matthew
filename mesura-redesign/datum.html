Why measure

Malta uses AI more than most of Europe. Readiness is a different measurement.

Using a tool is the easy part. Readiness asks whether your strategy, data, skills and safeguards can support what comes next. That is what Mesura measures.

How it works

Three stations. About ten minutes.

No preparation and no cost. You answer, Mesura measures, and you leave with a report you can act on.

  1. ST.1

    Answer

    A short set of questions about how you, or your organisation, work with AI today. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. ST.2

    Measure

    Your answers become a score for each of six dimensions, plus one overall reading of your readiness.

  3. ST.3

    Compare and act

    See each score against the Malta average and the EU. Your personalised report ranks the next steps that close your biggest gaps.

What we measure

Six dimensions. One reading.

Readiness is more than having the right tools. A gap in any one of these holds the other five back, so the survey looks at all six.

Strategy STR

A clear view of where AI creates value, and someone accountable for getting there.

Who is accountable for how your organisation uses AI?

Data DAT

Data that is findable, well kept and safe to use with AI tools.

Could you gather the data an AI project needs within a week?

Technology TEC

Tools and infrastructure that let people use AI securely and at the right scale.

Do people have approved AI tools they can use today?

Skills SKL

People who know how to use AI well, and when not to.

How confident is your team at checking an AI answer?

Governance GOV

Policies, risk checks and compliance, including the EU AI Act.

Do you have written rules for using AI with company data?

Culture CUL

Leaders and teams who try things, share what works and keep going.

When someone finds a better way with AI, does it spread?

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Something else? Write to support@mesura.ai.

Q.01Is it really free?

Yes. The survey and your personalised report are free.

Q.02How long does it take?

About ten minutes. You don’t need to prepare anything before you start.

Q.03What does the Malta average show?

For each dimension it shows the average score of respondents in Malta, so you can see where you are ahead and where you are behind. The EU benchmark gives the same comparison at European level.

Q.04Can I take it for myself rather than my company?

Yes. Choose “Myself” before you start and your report focuses on your own readiness.

Q.05What happens to my answers?

Your answers build your report and, anonymised, the benchmarks. The privacy policy has the details.
