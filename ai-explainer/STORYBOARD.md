# "Raising an AI" · How ChatGPT-style AI works, explained for parents

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920), 60 fps, 81 s, loops seamlessly. Built for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
**Audience:** busy parents (written for a family with 3 kids), watching on a phone, often with the sound off.
**Big idea:** *Raising an AI is weirdly like raising a kid.* Every technical step gets a parenting analogy,
so viewers have a line to quote and a reason to send it to another parent.

## Art direction

- **Palette:** Cream `#F4EDE1`, Ink `#15120F`, Tomato `#FF4D2E`, Cobalt `#2E4BFF`, Sun `#FFC53D`,
  Mint `#16C79A`, Lilac `#B7A6FF`, Night `#0C0B1A`. The background colour changes with each chapter.
- **Type:** Inter Tight (800–900) for the main kinetic lines. Instrument Serif Italic marks the emotional words.
  JetBrains Mono is used for data readouts and Caveat for "handwritten" notes.
- **Character:** "Bub", a cobalt chat-bubble character with an antenna. It appears in every chapter
  (reading glasses, a gold star sticker, a thought bubble, sunglasses) so viewers remember it.
- **Motion language:** springy overshoots, masked type reveals, match cuts (a period becomes the next
  background, dials become stars), and camera push-ins with shake on impacts.

## Fact check (kept deliberately conservative)

- Frontier models train on well over 10 trillion words (15T+ tokens is now common). At 250 words a minute,
  10 trillion words is about 76,000 years of reading nonstop, so the video says "75,000+ years".
- A child hears tens of millions of words in their first few years (BabyLM uses 100M as the budget for a 13-year-old).
- Large models have hundreds of billions of parameters (the "dials") and often dozens to 100+ layers.
- Training is next-word prediction repeated over trillions of words. Afterwards, human feedback (RLHF) teaches the model
  helpful, honest behaviour. Reasoning models think step by step before answering.
