05 Dial · 1960s · the mark as a knob

Trust the instrument you can hold.

The measuring tools people trust are the ones they can hold: a spirit level, a stopwatch, a thermostat that clicks into place. Dial imagines Mesura as an object in the tradition of mid-century industrial design. Honest materials, one coloured key, one knob. The opening in the mark finally does what it always looked like it should. It points.
