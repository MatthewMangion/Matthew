How long does the assessment take?

About ten minutes. The questions are in plain language and you don't need to prepare anything.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The assessment and your personalised report are free.

What are the six dimensions?

Strategy and leadership, data, technology, people and skills, governance and risk, and adoption and culture. Together they cover what it takes to use AI well, beyond the tools themselves.

Who is it for?

Anyone in a Maltese organisation, from a sole trader to a large company or public body. You can take it for yourself or on behalf of your organisation.

How does the benchmark work?

Your scores sit on the same 0–100 scale as the Malta average and an EU benchmark, so you can see where you lead and where you lag. Your report explains how each benchmark is built.

What happens to my answers?

They're used to produce your report and, anonymised, to keep the Malta benchmark current. The privacy policy sets out how they're stored and used.

Who runs Mesura?

Mesura is operated by SeyTravel Ltd. For anything else, email support@mesura.ai.
