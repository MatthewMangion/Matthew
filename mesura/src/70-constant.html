Since May 2019, every SI unit has been defined by a fixed constant of nature. The kilogram no longer depends on a metal cylinder kept in a vault outside Paris. It can be reproduced anywhere, with the right instrument. Constant imagines Mesura as that instrument: calibrated, luminous and exact. The mark becomes an aperture. Readiness is read clockwise from the opening, and light passes through it.
