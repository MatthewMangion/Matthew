06 Datum · 2010s · the mark as a chart

Every point is a measurement.

Dataism, a term popularised by the historian Yuval Noah Harari, describes a world that values anything by the data it produces. Datum takes the idea at its word, with Mesura’s restraint. Every element in the system is a measurement, the logo included. The mark has eight points, so here they are eight dimensions, drawn to scale from each organisation’s results. The opening turns to face the dimension with the most room to grow.
