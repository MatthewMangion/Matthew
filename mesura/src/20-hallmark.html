02 Hallmark · 1300 onwards · the mark as a seal

Measured, then marked.

Since 1300, silver in England has been tested and struck with a hallmark before sale. The mark says it has been measured and is what it claims to be. Hallmark gives Mesura’s report the same standing. The eight-point mark becomes the punch, struck into every certificate of readiness.
