    Questions

    Ask us anything.

    How long does it take?

    About ten minutes. The questions are in plain language and you don't need to prepare anything.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. The assessment and your personalised report are free.

    What are the six dimensions?

    Strategy and leadership, data, technology, people and skills, governance and risk, and adoption and culture.

    Who is it for?

    Anyone in a Maltese organisation, from a sole trader to a large company or public body. Take it for yourself or on behalf of your organisation.

    How does the benchmark work?

    Your scores share one 0–100 scale with the Malta average and an EU benchmark, so you can see where you lead and where you lag. Your report explains how each is calculated.

    What happens to my answers?

    They produce your report and, anonymised, keep the Malta benchmark current. The privacy policy sets out how they are stored and used.

    Is the quick read the same as my score?

    No. The quick read uses three questions for a rough first look. Your full score covers all six dimensions and comes with a report.
