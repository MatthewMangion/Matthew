Questions

Before you begin.

How long does it take?

About ten minutes. The questions are in plain language and need no preparation.

Is it really free?

Yes. The assessment and your personalised report are free.

What are the six dimensions?

Strategy and leadership, data, technology, people and skills, governance and risk, and adoption and culture.

Who is it for?

Anyone in a Maltese organisation, from a sole trader to a large company or public body. Take it for yourself or on behalf of your organisation.

How is the benchmark built?

Your scores share one 0–100 scale with the Malta average and an EU benchmark. Your report explains how each one is calculated.

What happens to my answers?

They produce your report and, anonymised, keep the Malta benchmark current. The privacy policy sets out how they are stored and used.

Is this a certification?

No. Mesura is a benchmark and a plan. The seal on your report shows your score; it is not a certificate of compliance.

Who runs Mesura?

Mesura is operated by SeyTravel Ltd. Write to support@mesura.ai with any question.
