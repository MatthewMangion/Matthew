## 01 · AI at work

One in five European companies now uses AI. A year earlier it was 13.5%, so that is almost 50%
growth in one year.

In Malta, 29.5% of people already use generative AI for work, nearly twice the EU average.

National numbers show that it's happening. They can't show where it's happening in your
organisation. Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

Source: Eurostat, 2025 (enterprises with at least 10 employees; individual GenAI dataset).

`#AI #GenerativeAI #FutureOfWork #Malta #AIReadiness #WorkforceDevelopment`

## 02 · Shadow AI

29.5% of people in Malta use generative AI for work. 21.6% of Maltese companies report using AI
at all.

The space in between has a name: shadow AI. That means unapproved tools, unrecorded workflows,
sensitive data in consumer apps and outputs nobody verified.

It isn't misconduct. It's people trying to work faster. But you can't manage what you can't see.
Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

Source: Eurostat, 2025. People and companies are measured in separate surveys.

`#ShadowAI #AIGovernance #ResponsibleAI #Compliance #Malta #FutureOfWork`

## 03 · Who is more ready?

Employee A uses AI every day. Employee B uses it once a week. Who is more ready for AI?

The answer depends on what you measure. Usage alone can't show readiness. Mesura scores six
dimensions of it, including what people actually know, and every answer is anonymous. Get your
Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

## 04 · Did it work?

You ran the AI training. Did it work?

Progress needs two measurements: a baseline before you act, and a remeasurement after. The
difference between them is your evidence. Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

`#AITraining #LearningAndDevelopment #HR #PeopleAnalytics #AIReadiness #FutureOfWork`

## 05 · Six dimensions

How do you measure AI readiness? Mesura scores six dimensions: Awareness, Adoption, Literacy,
Organisation, Affinity for Technology Interaction (ATI) and Demonstrated.

Five are self-reported and one is tested. Each is scored from 0 to 100, then weighted into one
readiness score. Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

`#AIReadiness #AILiteracy #PeopleAnalytics #HR #FutureOfWork #WorkforceDevelopment`

## 06 · Compared to what?

You scored 88%. Is that good? It depends who you compare with.

Mesura reads every score against three baselines: your sector, the Mesura average and your own
organisation. All six dimensions get the same comparisons, and the arithmetic sits next to every
score. No black box. Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.

`#Benchmarking #AIReadiness #PeopleAnalytics #HR #DataDriven #FutureOfWork`

## 07 · What you get

One assessment gives you:

- a readiness score and a named tier
- your strongest area, and where to focus
- all six dimensions, benchmarked against your sector and the Mesura median
- named actions, tied to the answers that lost points
- a scheduled remeasurement: proof that what you did made a difference

Get your Diagnostic at mesura.ai.
