# Mesura reels

Three vertical motion-graphics reels for Instagram and TikTok, built in the Mesura design system. Each one opens with a different hook, so you can find out which angle your audience responds to. Each is a seamless loop, and each runtime is one of the headline figures.

| Reel | Runtime | Hook | Built for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flagship** | 29.6 s (Malta's share of workers using generative AI) | "Malta ranks first in the EU." | The full story. Pride, then the turn, then the payoff. |
| **Guess the number** | 15.1 s (the EU average) | A slot counter spins under "Guess before it stops." | Comments. Viewers post their guess. |
| **Shadow AI** | 21.6 s (organisations that formally measure it) | "Your dashboard tracks everything. Except this." | Leaders. It frames the problem inside their own dashboard. |

All three are 1080 × 1920 with burned-in type (they read with the sound off), an original soundtrack scored to the picture, and a cut that loops back into its own first frame.

Upload the **60 fps** files to TikTok. Instagram accepts either; use the 30 fps file if the app flags the upload or the motion looks uneven after processing.

## Posting plan

1. **Start with Guess the number.** Comment prompts do the most for early distribution, and the comments give the next two posts social proof.
2. **Post the Flagship two or three days later** and pin it to the top of the profile. It's the full story, and the one to point people to.
3. **Follow with Shadow AI** for decision-makers. It's also the strongest candidate for LinkedIn, where the same 9:16 file plays well on mobile.
4. **Test hooks before you spend.** On Instagram, post each one first as a Trial Reel (shown to non-followers only), compare three-second hold and completion, then boost the winner. On TikTok, compare average watch time and the share of views that loop.
5. **Timing.** For a business audience in Malta, weekday mornings between 07:30 and 09:00 and lunchtime between 12:00 and 13:30 (CET) are good starting points. Check your own analytics after the first two posts.
6. **Reply fast.** Answer the first hour of comments. On TikTok, reply to the best guesses with video replies. On Instagram, share each reel to Stories with a poll sticker: "Does your organisation measure AI use? Yes / Not yet".
7. **Covers.** Set the cover image from `renders/covers/` on upload. The three are designed to sit together as a row on your grid (paper, pine, dark).

**On audio.** The scores are original and every effect is synced to the picture, so post them as they are. If you want a trending sound for reach on TikTok, add it in the app at low volume under the original audio rather than replacing it.

## Before you publish

- **Check the figures.** 29.6%, 15.1% and 21.6% come from the Mesura design-system document, attributed to Eurostat (2025). Confirm them against the source before posting. "Rank 01 / 27" counts the 27 EU member states.
- **Product screens are illustrative.** The survey questions, the "72 / 100" readiness score, the benchmark of 58, the dimension scores and the dashboard KPIs are mock-ups. They carry a "Sample report" badge where the report appears. Swap in real screens if you'd rather.
- **Put the link in your bio.** The end cards carry the mesura.ai logo and "Link in bio", so the Diagnostic link needs to be in the profile before you post.

## How it's made

Everything here is code, and nothing is stock or template.

- **Engine** (`src/lib.js`, `src/engine.js`). A deterministic canvas renderer: every frame is a pure function of time, so frames render in parallel and any moment can be scrubbed. Motion blur is real. Each output frame averages 6 to 16 sub-frame samples across a 180° shutter, with more samples during whips and slams.
- **Logo**. Traced from the mesura.ai artwork into vector paths (`tools/trace_logo.py` → `src/logo.js`). It matches the original to sub-pixel accuracy (alpha IoU 0.993) and uses the artwork's own colours: gold #E1A23C, teal #073D44, and a cream wordmark on dark grounds. The mark only rotates as part of an entrance and always settles at its true orientation.
- **Typography**. Source Serif 4 is baked into static optical-size-60 instances so display type uses the high-contrast display cut. Inter and JetBrains Mono carry labels and data. Glyph positions come from kerned prefix measurement, and words rise through masks.
- **Kit** (`src/kit.js`). Brand components drawn from the design system: odometer and slot counters with independent digit columns, a gauge ring, dimension lines and rulers, the waffle chart, a sundial-style shadow terminator with a rim light, the Mesura mark as a rising sun, a dashboard, survey and report screens, a strip-based 3D perspective projector for the card flips, and container-transform buttons.
- **Reels** (`src/reels/*.js`). The choreography, authored in beats so every cut lands on the music. The flagship runs 60 beats in 29.6 s (≈ 121.6 BPM).
- **Score** (`tools/compose.py`). A numpy synthesiser. Drums, a ticking "clock" motif, sidechained bass and pads, a dotted-eighth ping-pong pluck and FM bells make the music. Risers, impacts, whooshes, slot-machine clicks, typewriter keys and UI taps are designed from the reel's own cue sheet, so each one hits its frame. It includes a tape stop and per-section gain automation, and is mastered for phone speakers.
- **Renderer** (`tools/render.cjs`). Four headless Chromium pages render in parallel into lossless segments, then ffmpeg encodes a BT.709-tagged delivery file.

### Rebuild

Requirements: Node 18+ with Playwright (Chromium), Python 3 with `numpy scipy pillow imageio-ffmpeg` (and `potracer` to re-trace the logo).

```bash
tools/build.sh flagship    # cues → score → 60 fps + 30 fps masters → cover
tools/build.sh guess
tools/build.sh shadow

# preview and scrub in the browser (serve the folder, then open /studio.html)
npx http-server . -p 8080
# stills and contact sheets for review
node tools/render.cjs --reel flagship --sheet 0.5
```
